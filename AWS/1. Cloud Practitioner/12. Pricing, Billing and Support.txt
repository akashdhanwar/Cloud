CLOUD COSTS - THE BIGGER PICTURE 

TCO -   Total Cost of Ownership(Not only running cost but also infra cost)

Traditional Data Centre                             |       Cloud
-   Server(May expand)                              |       -   No Cost(Pay as you go)
-   Design and Maintain System                      |       -   NA
-   Utilities                                       |       -   NA
-   Developers                                      |       -   Developers
-   Disaster Downtime                               |       -   NA

Six Advantages of Cloud Computing 
-   Trade fixed expense for variable expense    -   Paying for only what you use 
-   Benefits from massive economies of scale    
-   Stop Guessing capacity                      -   Auto Scaling
-   Increase Speed and Agility                  
-   Stop spending money maintaining data centers
-   Go Global in minutes

Fixed vs Variable Expense       -   Fixed expense - fixed servers for peak payload. 
                                    Variable expense - stop guessing capacity

Economies of Scale              -   AWS aggregates usage from hundreds of thousands of customers
                                    These economies of scale allow AWS to offer low pay as-you-go prices 

Opportunity Cost and the Cloud  -   Deliver value faster - Focusing on application development rather than infrastructure development can give you a competitive edge 
                                    Expand Globally      - Quickly span the globe without the need for global work forces and infrastructures
                                    
The Well Architected Framework  -   Security                -   Focuses on protection of data, systems and any assets used by your workload(Principle of least privilidge, Securing data at rest, In-transit)
                                                                Eg - Use CloudTrail to log all actions performed in your account
                                    Cost Optimization       -   Focuses on ongoing process of maintaining costs in the cloud(Right Sizing, Implementing Lifecycle)
                                                                Eg - Use S3 Intelligent-Tiering to automatically move data
                                    Performance Efficiency  -   Focuses on the ability to use computing resources efficiently to meet requirements(Maximizing Resource usage)
                                                                Eg - Using Lambda to ruun code with zero administration
                                    Operational Excellence  -   Focuses on creating application that successfully support your workload(Event Management)
                                                                Eg - Use CodeCommit for code and template version control
                                    Reliability             -   Focuses on architecting a workload to be consistent and able to recover quickly(Disaster Recovery, Network Topology)
                                                                Eg - Use Multi-AZ deployments of RDS databases
                                    Sustainability          -   Focuses on environmental impacts like energy efficiency and consumption(Region selection)
                                                                Eg - Use EC2 Auto Scaling to ensure maximum utilization
