- Region
Region is a physical location in the world that consists of 2 or more AZ
3 or more as per latest definition

- AZ 
Data Center is a building filled with servers
AZ is one or more discrete data centers - each with redundant power, networking and connectivity - housed in separate facilities

- Edge Locations
Edge Locations are endpoints for AWS that are used for caching content
Typically this consists of CloudFront(Amazon CDN)
There are many more edge locations than regions
